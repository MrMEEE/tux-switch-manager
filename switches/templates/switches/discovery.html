Start a discovery

Python SSH and HTTP/HTTPS probes look for network-vendor fingerprints or SSH on the NETCONF port. If no vendor fingerprint is found, local MAC/OUI lookup can identify a known network manufacturer on a directly connected subnet. Open ports alone are excluded. No sudo, OS fingerprinting, default passwords or SNMP communities are used. Candidates may also be servers, routers or access points; authenticated verification is required.
